About

I'm a medical student at Vanvitelli in Naples, working toward a career in diagnostic imaging. My interest in radiology and nuclear medicine is what shapes most of the decisions in my training — which electives I take, which hospitals I want exposure to, and which country I eventually want to specialise in.

Before medicine, I worked as an English tutor, which is where a lot of my comfort with teaching and explaining complex material comes from — something I expect to carry into clinical teaching later on.

My long-term plan is to build toward a residency in Berlin, using the years ahead to gain real exposure to German clinical training well before I apply — through structured exchanges and, later, targeted clinical placements in the specialty itself.
